Hisar privacy policy

Applies to the Hisar desktop app, the Hisar browser extension and the Hisar mobile apps. Last updated 9 October 2026.

Summary

Hisar is a self-hosted password manager for one family and their friends. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Where your data goes

  • Your vaults are encrypted on your own devices before they leave them (end-to-end encryption). The Hisar server, run by your family's administrator, stores only encrypted data and cannot read your passwords, notes or other items.
  • Your master password and Secret Key never leave your devices.
  • The server stores what it needs to run your account: your email address and name, your devices' names and when they were last seen, and the encrypted vault data.

The browser extension

  • The extension talks only to the Hisar desktop app on the same computer, through the browser's native messaging. It sends no data to the internet.
  • It reads the address of the page you are on and the login forms on it, to offer matching logins and to offer saving a login after you sign in. Page contents are not stored or sent anywhere except to the Hisar app on your computer.
  • It stores only a random installation ID, the pairing token for your Hisar app, its settings, and the sites you chose "Never for this site" for, in the browser's local extension storage.
  • Logins are filled only on the site they were saved for.

Third parties

Hisar uses no third-party services. Data is not transferred to anyone other than your own Hisar server.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes your data from a device. To delete your account and its data from the server, ask your Hisar administrator.
